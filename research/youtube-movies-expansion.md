# YouTube Movies Expansion Research Progress

Started: 2026-11-16  
Goal: 100 new qualifying records beyond the 234-record baseline.  
Current catalog after the latest research batch: 264 movies; YouTube Movies category: 41 records.

## Research method and limitations

No YouTube Data API credentials or connector are available. The available web interface does not provide complete channel archive pagination, so this work uses indexed public channel/video results, official distributor catalogues and title-level distributor sources. The research is title-by-title but is not a complete enumeration of the historical uploads on any channel. Search indexing and distributor pages can be stale; the report does not claim playback, region availability or embed permission was independently tested for every video.

For acceptance, each record has an exact YouTube video ID found in an official distributor channel result and a title-specific distributor catalogue page or collection entry. A verified channel by itself was not treated as rights evidence. Publication timestamps were omitted because indexed results exposed calendar dates without a reliable timezone-qualified timestamp.
